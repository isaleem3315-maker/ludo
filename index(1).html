How to play

Start: roll a 6 to bring a piece out.

Move: clockwise around the board, then up your colored lane to the center. Exact roll to finish.

Capture: land on an opponent to send them home. Stars and start squares are safe.

Kill to enter home: capture once before your pieces may enter the home lane (host can turn this off).

Roll again: after a 6, a capture or reaching home. Three 6s lose the turn.
