How to play

🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a piece out.

➡️ Move clockwise, then up your colored lane to the center.

💥 Land on an opponent to send them home — except on ⭐ and start squares.

🔁 A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.

🏆 Get all 4 pieces home. You need the exact roll to finish.

Congratulations! 🎉
